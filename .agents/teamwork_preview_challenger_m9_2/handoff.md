## 3. Caveats

- **JPEG Color Quantization**: In lossy JPEG keyframes, exact color matches for `#f59e0b` shift by $\Delta E \approx 1\text{--}4$ due to 4:2:0 chroma subsampling. The system properly accounts for this by applying an empirical color tolerance $\le 24.0$, which reliably detects the amber stroke.
- **PIL Large Image Load**: In Python 3.14, sequential decompression of large PNGs benefits from `cv2.imread` or `ImageFile.LOAD_TRUNCATED_IMAGES = True` to handle EOF zero-byte metadata gracefully.
